Before you book

What information should I provide?

Share your vehicle make and model, the symptoms you have noticed, and any relevant service history or warning lights.

Can I bring a 4WD / 4x4 vehicle?

The company information supplied for this website indicates that services are available for standard and 4WD/4x4 vehicles. Mention your vehicle when requesting a booking.

How do I confirm an appointment?

Send a booking request through the website or call the workshop. The team can discuss timing and the vehicle requirement directly with you.
